# v1.5.81 Master Preview Cancellation and Native Result Isolation

## Fixed defects

- A 15-second master-preview render could continue after settings invalidation, queue clearing, or track removal. Its late decoder, finalizer, encoder, or Blob URL result could overwrite newer state and retain large audio buffers longer than necessary.
- Master-preview work now has a dedicated job owner, `AbortController`, unique generation ID, and active-track assertion. Superseded and detached jobs cannot commit status, URLs, waveforms, or Dock autoplay state.
- The decode service, master finalizer worker, and WAV encoder worker receive the same cancellation signal. Queue clearing, track removal, resource release, and preview invalidation abort the complete pipeline.
- A URL created immediately before a stale commit is revoked locally instead of entering track state.
- Global busy and rendering identifiers are cleared only by the job that owns them, preventing an older `finally` block from clearing a newer operation.
- Native share or direct-save rejection arriving after the download-assist panel was closed or replaced is ignored. It no longer raises a stale cancellation toast or changes controls belonging to a disposed panel.
