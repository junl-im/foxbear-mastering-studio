# v1.5.98 Worker Safe Retry, Health Levels, and Resource Stress

## Recovery boundary

A Worker transfer list may detach its ArrayBuffers. Retrying the same low-level payload is therefore unsafe. The recovery coordinator accepts only high-level jobs that can be rebuilt from retained track files:

- track analysis
- full mastering
- 15-second master preview

ZIP and general export work is not automatically retried because repeating a save operation can produce duplicate downloads.

## Health levels

- Normal: recent progress and moderate transfer memory
- Watch: at least eight seconds without progress, high transfer memory, or multiple concurrent workers
- Danger: at least fifteen seconds without progress, very high transfer memory, failed runtime health, severe heap pressure, or a long main-thread task

## Resource regression

The v1.5.98 smoke test verifies 30 track cleanup cycles, 90 Blob URL revocations, 60 abort-owner releases, 30 AudioContext closures, bounded Worker history, and zero active Worker transfer accounting after recovery.
