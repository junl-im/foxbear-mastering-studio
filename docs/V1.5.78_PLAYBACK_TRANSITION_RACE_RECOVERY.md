# v1.5.78 Playback Transition Race Recovery

## Problem

Playback fades were cancelled by removing their animation frame, but the Promise returned by the old fade was never settled. Rapid source changes could therefore retain closures and audio elements. More critically, a cancelled fade-out still continued through its `.then()` chain and could call `pause()` after a newer play request had already started.

## Fix

- Store a small fade controller on each audio element.
- On replacement, cancel the pending RAF, clear the controller, and resolve the superseded fade with `false`.
- Ignore stale RAF callbacks whose controller no longer owns the audio element.
- Run `pause()` and crossfade completion cleanup only when every fade completed successfully.
- Clear RAF and controller references after both normal completion and cancellation.

## Verification

`qa/v1578_playback_transition_race_recovery_smoke.js` runs the playback service in a VM with deterministic animation frames and verifies:

1. A replaced fade resolves as cancelled rather than remaining pending.
2. A rapid pause-then-play sequence does not execute the stale pause.
3. A cancelled crossfade does not pause the previous source or report successful completion.
4. Completed fades leave no controller or RAF reference on the audio element.
