# v1.6.2 Non-Blocking Health Status and Design Polish

## Problems found

- Routine import, decode, mastering, render, and automatic wake-lock activity was counted as a warning even when the app was operating normally.
- A historical long task or decode error could keep the health state in warning mode long after recovery.
- Users had to open a large diagnostics modal to discover health state, while a persistent normal indicator would add unnecessary visual noise.
- The normal diagnostics view displayed empty recommendation and Worker sections plus disabled recovery controls.

## Resolution

- Split informational activities from actionable warnings.
- Apply recent windows to transient long-task, decode, and wake-lock failures.
- Keep normal state visually silent and show a small settings badge only for watch or danger.
- Show a compact, non-blocking notice only after danger is confirmed twice; remove it after two recovered samples.
- Keep the full panel manual and hide empty or unavailable sections.
