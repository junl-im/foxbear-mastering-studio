# v1.6.3 Health Acknowledgement and Settings Summary

## Problems found

- The decode activity code was added twice to the same diagnostic summary.
- A dismissed danger notice could immediately return after a reload even when the condition had not changed.
- Settings showed only a generic diagnostics action, so users could not see the current reason without opening the full modal.
- Watch and danger badges could flicker after a single transient sample.
- The bottom health notice and the toast stack could occupy the same mobile screen area.

## Resolution

- Count decode activity once.
- Persist acknowledgement of the same danger condition for 30 minutes while keeping the settings badge visible.
- Show normal, watch, or danger plus a concise current reason inside Settings.
- Require two consecutive watch or danger samples and two healthy samples before changing the visible ambient state.
- Measure the visible toast stack and reserve its height under the health notice.
