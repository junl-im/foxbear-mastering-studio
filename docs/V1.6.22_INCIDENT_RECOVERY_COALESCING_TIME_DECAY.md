# v1.6.22 Incident Recovery Coalescing and Time Decay

- Simultaneous online, network-change, and long-resume recovery requests share one in-flight sweep.
- Stronger pending requirements are merged and executed once after the active sweep.
- Recovery results expose merged reasons for anonymous operational diagnostics.
- Callable and Hosting route scores decay by 15% per elapsed day, capped at eight steps, so old network history naturally loses influence.
- Current cooldown and non-transient error visibility remain unchanged.
